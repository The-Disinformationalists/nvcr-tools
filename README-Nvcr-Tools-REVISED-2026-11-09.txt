nvcr-tools
==========

The nvcr-tools repo contains files for use by nonviolent civil resistance workers and organizations. These are tools and other resources created for a variety of purposes: to help establish or protect democratic governments, resist oppressive forces, verify or fact check statements or imagery, and communicate nonviolently with persons within those movements, and with opposing forces and the public at large (by which I mean the global public).

What is in this repo
--------------------

Current files:

US_Statistical_Agencies.xlsx - A reference list of principal federal statistical agencies and major federal statistical programs in the United States that report statistical information to the public.

Sheet 1: US Statistical Agencies - 55 agencies/programs with agency name, verified URL, description / mandate, standardized lag time between reporting dates, media contact name, agency telephone number, agency email for media contacts, and notes.

Sheet 2: Read Me - Scope, column definitions, and limitations for the file.

This file is intended to help users find authoritative U.S. statistics quickly and understand how long after a reference period an agency typically publishes its data.

Planned files:

Additional tools and resources for nonviolent civil resistance work will be added over time, including tools for verification, fact-checking, secure communication, and documentation.

How to use this repo
--------------------

1. Browse the files in this repo on GitHub.
2. Download any file you need by opening it and clicking Download, or clone the whole repo with: git clone https://github.com/jpstarpaper/nvcr-tools.git
3. For spreadsheets, open them in Excel, Google Sheets, or LibreOffice.

Notes on the statistical agencies file
-------------------------------------

Scope: The list covers the 13 principal federal statistical agencies recognized by the U.S. Office of Management and Budget plus other major federal statistical programs that regularly publish statistics.

Verified URLs are .gov domains where available.

Media contact names change frequently and many agencies do not publish a single named contact. NA means Not Available / no single named media contact published. Use the agency press office email/phone instead.

Lag times are standardized schedules, not guarantees. Shutdowns, revisions, and data quality reviews can shift dates.

Contributing and use
--------------------

This repo is public for viewing and downloading. If you want to suggest a correction, addition, or new tool, please open an Issue in this repo with details and sources.

Please verify critical information against the original agency source before relying on it for publication or action.
